### SECTION A. DESCRIPTION OF PROJECT 

**A.1 Purpose and general description of project**

>> This project aims to reduce the use of the firewood, hence, the carbon emission

that leads to improvement in the indoor air quality.This project activity is developed in

Maharashtra state of India, but further the distribution will be taken in the different

states of India. The project is located within the project boiundary of host country India.

The households in the rural villages of Maharashtra state mostly rely on the tradtional

mudstoves and also some of the households uses three stone open fire with firewood

as a fuel for their cooking needs. The improved cookstove reduces the consumption of

firewood for cooking and reducing the smoke in the environment.

The project activity is eligible under the methodology of TPDDTEC (Technolgies and the

Practises to Displace Decentralized Thermal Energy Consumption) Version 4.0. The

project is implemented by IICARE Foundation that involves the activity of distribution

of 15,500 Improved Cookstove (ICS) in rural households The project boundary is

considered the physical boundary of the host country India.

The practice of cooking on traditional coookstoves in rural villages was found to be the

baseline scenario of the project that poses significant health and environmental risks

that can also be avoided by distributing access to fuel-efficient cookstoves.among the

community by distributing these improved cookstoves, the project aims to provide

communities with various different benefits, including poverty reduction, improvement

in health, increased productivity, and also to mitigate climate change. These cookstoves

can help in improving the health of women, children, and other household members.

Besides mitigating climate change by reducing CO 2 emissions from the project, this

project will deliver numerous benefits to local communities and environment including:

A.1.2. Legal ownership of products generated by the project and legal rights to
alter use of resources required to service the project

>>The Project Developer possesses the rightful legal claim to the emission reduction

offsets that result from the operations of this Project Activity. To prevent any potential

claims by other stakeholders, comprehensive safeguards are enacted. These measures

take the form of well-defined categorical declarations and contractual agreements,

mutually agreed upon between the Project Developer and relevant stakeholder entities.

These agreements ensure the transfer of rights concerning emission reductions and

other impact assets exclusively in favor of the Project Developer. This means that no

other stakeholders have any legal entitlement to claim these rights.

As a crucial follow-through, the emission reduction entitlements generated by the

project are formally and concretely conveyed from the beneficiary households to the

project Developer. This transfer of rights is documented through a signed copy of the

associated declaration forms that are an integral part of the distribution of the

cookstoves.

These simple procedures emphasize our commitments to transparent, accountable, and

legally sound processes throughout the project lifecycle

A.3 Technologies and/or measures

>> This project involves the distribution of cookstoves within diffrent rural places of

Maharashtra in India. This fuel efficient improved cookstoves has replaced the use of

traditional mud cookstoves

The stove design is tailored to align with the essential requisites outlined in section A.4.

Specifically, the selected model,in the project of "–Eco Mini Model 04" stove, embodies

the following key attributes:

-  **Single Fed (Single Combustion Chamber):** Designed to optimize combustion

efficiency within a single chamber, enabling optimal heat generation and minimizing

emissions.

-  **Portability:** Is light in weight of 3.3 kg  and portable to travel .

-  **Fuels:** Compatible with various woody biomass materials, encompassing fuelwood..

-  **Efficiency:** The improved cookstove Eco mini model 04 stove has an efficiency of

36.42%.

-  **Life Expectancy:** It is Designed for longevity, with a life expectancy exceeding for

7 years.

-  **Almost Smokeless:** Designed to significantly reduce smoke emissions and

enhancing indoor air quality making cooking easier.
